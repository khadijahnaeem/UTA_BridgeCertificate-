About the Certificate

Preparing Engineers for the Bridge Industry

Specialized education for engineers entering or advancing within the bridge engineering profession.

UTA offers the only Bridge Engineering Certificate in Texas, providing engineers with specialized knowledge for careers in State Departments of Transportation, public agencies, and private consulting firms.

The program emphasizes practical engineering knowledge and professional applications so that participants can develop skills that can be used directly in bridge design, analysis, inspection, construction, and asset management.

Curriculum

Course Offerings

11 courses offered. 6 courses required to complete the certificate.

01
Design

Design of Concrete Bridges

Introduces the design of prestressed and reinforced concrete superstructures in accordance with AASHTO LRFD Bridge Design Specifications.

02
Design

Design of Steel Bridges

Covers the structural design of steel plate girder and rolled beam bridges per the AASHTO LRFD Bridge Design Specifications.

03
Design

Bridge Substructures

Addresses the analysis and design of bridge foundations and supporting structures.

04
Analysis & Digital Tools

Bridge Software

Provides applied training in industry-standard bridge engineering software suites. Students utilize various software and standard Excel VBA macros for automated design, load rating, and structural analysis.

05
Design

Design of Railroad Bridges

Introduces the specific design criteria and structural behaviors unique to light rail and heavy rail transit bridges, guided by the AREMA Manual for Railway Engineering.

06
Analysis & Digital Tools

Bridge Geometry, Fit, and BIM

Integrates roadway geometry with 3D Digital Bridge Modeling (BrIM).

07
Analysis & Digital Tools

Numerical Modeling and Analysis for Bridges

Explores advanced finite element analysis (FEA) techniques for complex structural evaluation.

08
Field & Lifecycle

Bridge Inspection, Evaluation, and Asset Management

Details condition assessment protocols, diagnostic load testing evaluations, and the integration of inspection data into GIS-based databases for lifecycle asset management, vulnerability assessments, and strategic rehabilitation planning.

09
Field & Lifecycle

Bridge Constructability, Phasing, and Detailing

Focuses on the practical execution of bridge projects in the field. Topics include maintenance of traffic, staged construction sequencing, temporary works design, and the rigorous quality control of standard bridge sheets and layouts.

10
Field & Lifecycle

Non-Destructive Evaluation & Structural Health Monitoring

Covers the deployment of modern NDE technologies and sensor networks for condition assessment.

11
Design

Complex Bridge Design

Addresses the design and analysis of non-standard and highly constrained bridge configurations.

8 PDH per Course

Professional development hours are available for each completed course, with classes held on six consecutive Saturdays.
